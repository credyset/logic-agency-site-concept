The operating method

Diagnose. Define. Deliver.

This is not a three-part handoff. The same team carries context from diagnosis into decisions and through implementation as growth introduces new products, suppliers, channels, and constraints.

  1. 01Diagnose

    Find the constraint behind the symptom.

    We inspect the product, packaging, suppliers, costs, compliance requirements, workflows, and growth constraints—not just the issue that surfaced first.

    You receive

    • Current-state analysis
    • Risk and cost breakdown
    • Compliance gaps
    • Supplier assessment
  2. 02Define

    Turn findings into an executable operating plan.

    We translate what we find into decisions, specifications, budgets, owners, and decision gates so the next move is clear.

    You receive

    • Prioritized roadmap
    • Material and supplier decisions
    • Technical requirements
    • Timeline and budget
  3. 03Deliver

    Own the build and coordinate the handoffs.

    We own execution across product development, suppliers, factories, testing, and quality; coordinate logistics partners; and support retail readiness alongside the internal team.

    You receive

    • Vendor coordination
    • Sampling and production
    • Quality and compliance execution
    • Ongoing reporting

Advice is only useful if it survives contact with production. Logic stays for that part.

How the work stays controlled

Visible decisions. Explicit owners. Closed loops.

Operational change becomes reliable when the system makes risks, commitments, and unresolved issues easy to see.
