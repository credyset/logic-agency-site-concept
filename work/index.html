What the work reveals

The problem is rarely isolated.

  1. 01

    Growth changes the operating conditions.

    More volume, more SKUs, or broader distribution puts new pressure on suppliers, specifications, costs, and internal capacity.

  2. 02

    A visible failure points to a deeper constraint.

    A quality issue may be a supplier-capacity problem. A margin issue may begin with materials, packout, freight, or inventory decisions.

  3. 03

    Implementation is where the answer becomes real.

    Logic stays involved across specifications, suppliers, production, quality, and the handoffs required to put the recommendation into use.
