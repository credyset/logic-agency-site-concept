Three connected disciplines

What the team sells and delivers.

Each discipline can solve a defined problem. Their real value appears when the same team can see—and manage—the consequences across all three.

01Logic-owned

Product & Packaging Development

Turn the brand idea into a producible, cost-aware packaging system.

Packaging is a co-equal discipline—and often the clearest place to see how product decisions, margin, manufacturing, and supply chain performance connect.

  • Structural packaging and component development
  • Materials, finishes, fit, and form decisions
  • Specification development and cost engineering
  • Supplier sourcing, sampling, and qualification
  • Transit, compatibility, and performance testing
02Logic-owned

Manufacturing & Quality

Build the product with the right factory, standards, and controls.

We translate intent into manufacturing requirements, manage the path to production, and hold suppliers accountable to the approved cost, quality, and calendar.

  • Factory identification, vetting, and onboarding
  • Tooling, prototypes, samples, and pilot runs
  • Quality standards and inspection planning
  • Production oversight and corrective action
  • Landed-cost and supplier-performance analysis
03Coordinated

Supply Chain & Operations

Coordinate the handoffs that move product into the market reliably.

We coordinate logistics partners and operating workflows so freight, inventory, compliance, and fulfillment support growth instead of eroding margin.

  • Freight, customs, and 3PL coordination
  • Inventory planning and replenishment workflows
  • Vendor ownership and operating cadence
  • Retail packaging and compliance readiness
  • Risk planning, reporting, and issue resolution
